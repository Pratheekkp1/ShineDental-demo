Welcome to Shine Dental

At Shine Dental, we deliver painless, effective dental care that keeps your smile bright and beautiful. Dr. Si-Hyeon Lee tailors every treatment to your needs, ensuring a comfortable and positive dental experience for every patient.

About Us

Shine Dental is a modern, welcoming dental practice using the latest technology to provide gentle, patient-focused care. We treat each patient as family, emphasizing respect, compassion, and professionalism.

  • Exams, cleanings, and fillings for all ages
  • Orthodontics & Invisalign
  • Implants with CBCT & surgical guides
  • Bridges, crowns, dentures
  • Extractions & root canals
  • Cosmetic dentistry & whitening
  • Gum treatment
  • Emergency dental care

We also offer nitrous oxide for added comfort. Located in Winchester, VA, we proudly serve surrounding communities.
